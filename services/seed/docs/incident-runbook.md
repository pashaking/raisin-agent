# Incident runbook (internal, Raisin staff only)

Severity 1: payment gateway outage or suspected card data exposure. Page the on-call engineer, freeze deployments, open the incident channel, notify the CTO within 15 minutes. Severity 2: elevated decline rate above 20 percent for 10 minutes, or fraud score spikes. Check gateway status page, review WAF blocks, and compare against the dynamic IP block list. All incidents get a root cause analysis within five business days. This document is classified internal and is not available to charity administrators or donors.
